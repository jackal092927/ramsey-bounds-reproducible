# Primary sources and dependency map

This is a targeted source map, not an exhaustive novelty survey. Reopened primary HTML during this consolidation is distinguished from the earlier project's version-specific audits. New derivations in this folder are our deductions; the mere existence of a source theorem does not certify every use of it.

| Source | Version / role | Verification boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Lowe--Kim--Bondesan--Hayakawa](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.03278v1) | July 3, 2026 v1; Problem 9, Lemma 7, Section 7 | Reopened. Problem 9 is the true Betti-normalized statistic with two gaps and nonzero initial Betti number. Uniform-mixture access and overlap are additional containment assumptions. Unrestricted hardness remains a conjecture in this version. |

A radius-independent collision exponent is not a radius-independent algorithm. |

The minimum source-completion table for the main candidate has five entries: (i) concrete implementing sphere and attaching map for each active state; (ii) closure under the required fixed basis guards; (iii) fixed-gadget nullity and limiting kernel; (iv) local lifted-cycle spectrum and relative-bulk injectivity; (v) no cross-gadget simplices. The abstract quotient/min--max transfer and weighted-path inequalities are locally supplied. The named source-class bridge remains a separate theorem obligation.

The main novelty question is whether the exact multiplicity/naturality consequence already follows explicitly in the latest versions, and whether a restricted-circuit result has enough independent complexity content. Absence of identical terminology is not evidence of novelty.
