For an inclusion of clique complexes \(X_1\subseteq X_2\), the normalized persistence in degree \(d\) is the fraction \(\rank[H_d(X_1)\to H_d(X_2)]/\dim H_d(X_1)\) of the degree-\(d\) holes of \(X_1\) that survive in \(X_2\). Lowe, Kim, Bondesan, and Hayakawa asked for the complexity of estimating this fraction under inverse-polynomial spectral-gap promises on both endpoint Laplacians, conjectured hardness for a variant \(\SDQCone\) of \(\mathsf{DQC}_1\) with perfect completeness, and reduced the conjecture to an open kernel-preserving realization of history Hamiltonians by clique complexes.
We carry out that realization for exact circuits over \(G_2=\{X,\CX,\CCX,H\otimes H\}\) and draw three consequences, all valid for unweighted clique complexes.
(1)~Normalized persistence is hard for the perfect-subspace-fraction class \(\SF^{G_2}\), which contains \(\BQPone^{G_2}\) and the fraction form of \(\SDQCone^{G_2}\) used in the hardness proofs of Lowe et al.; the hard instances have \(2^{r}\) initial holes, of which the fraction \(\dim S_x/2^{r}\) survives, where \(S_x\) is the perfectly accepted subspace of the source verifier.
(2)~The hard instances satisfy the large-overlap and state-preparation conditions under which Lowe et al.\ place the problem in \(\BQP\), so on this family the problem is \(\BQPone^{G_2}\)-hard and in \(\BQP\).
(3)~Computing the exact Betti number of a clique complex promised to have an inverse-polynomial spectral gap is \(\sharpP\)-complete under weakly parsimonious reductions.
The engine is a finite-certificate transfer theorem. For a fixed palette of weighted clique gadgets satisfying finitely many exactly checkable local conditions, every unit chain \(x\) of the full geometric complex satisfies
\[
\dist(x,K)^2\le C\Bigl(t\lambda^2+\frac{\langle x,\Delta x\rangle}{g\lambda^{2p}}\Bigr),
\]
where \(K\) is the embedded kernel of the simulated projector Hamiltonian, \(g\) its gap, \(t\) the number of gadgets, \(\lambda\) the private vertex weight, and \(p\) the private depth of the certified fillings (\(p=5\) here). Hence the geometric Laplacian has exactly \(\dim K\) zero modes, a gap linear in \(g\) above them, and a harmonic space within \(O(\lambda\sqrt t)\) of \(K\); exact fillings identify the endpoint homology with a quotient \(V/W_A\) of the register cycle space, and nested term sets induce the natural quotient epimorphisms, so the persistent rank equals the later kernel dimension without any choice of compatible harmonic representatives.
