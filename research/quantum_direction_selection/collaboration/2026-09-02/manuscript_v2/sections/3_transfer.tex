\section{Finite-certificate transfer to the complete geometric kernel}
\label{sec:transfer}

We isolate finite local conditions under which a clique-gadget construction
controls every chain of the full geometric complex, including chains
supported mostly outside the simulated register.  The statement does not use
convergence of a spectral projector whose rank grows with the simulated
circuit.

\subsection{The certificate model}

Consider one active gadget before padding.  Its weight-one register is \(R_a\),
its target cycle space is \(V_a=Z_{d_a}(R_a)\), and every private vertex has
weight \(\lambda\).  In normalized chain coordinates put
\[
D_a(\lambda)=
\begin{bmatrix}\partial_{d_a}(\lambda)\\
\partial_{d_a+1}(\lambda)^*\end{bmatrix}
=D_{a,0}+\lambda D_{a,1},
\]
so that \(\Delta_{Y_a,d_a}(\lambda)=D_a(\lambda)^*D_a(\lambda)\).  The
decomposition is affine in \(\lambda\) because every entry of the normalized
boundary is \(0\), \(\pm1\) (a register vertex removed) or \(\pm\lambda\) (a
private vertex removed).  The value at \(\lambda=0\) is a matrix limit, not
a singular change of basis; \(D_{a,0}\) is the boundary pair of a rational
chain complex, since \(\partial(\lambda)^2=0\) for all \(\lambda\) forces
\(\partial_0^2=0\).

\begin{assumption}[Finite local certificate]
\label{ass:local-certificate}
For every member \(a\) of a fixed finite palette there are an orthogonal
subspace \(Q_a\) contained in the span of private simplex coordinates, an
output projector \(R_a^{\rm out}\), and a rank-one
projector \(\pi_a\) on \(V_a\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item \(B_{d_a}(Y_a)\cap V_a=\ran\pi_a\) for every \(\lambda>0\);
\item \(\ker D_{a,0}=V_a\oplus Q_a\), orthogonally;
\item for \(T_a(\lambda)=R_a^{\rm out}D_a(\lambda)\),
\[
T_a(\lambda)=\lambda T_{a,1},\qquad T_{a,1}V_a=0,\qquad
\|T_{a,1}q\|\ge b\|q\|\quad(q\in Q_a);
\]
\item every retained output coordinate contains a private vertex.
\end{enumerate}
The constant \(b>0\), the positive singular-value floor of \(D_{a,0}\), and
all matrix norms are uniform over the palette.
\end{assumption}

All data in \cref{ass:local-certificate} are finite integer or rational linear
algebra.  Exact fillings prove one inclusion in (i); matching boundary ranks
and surviving register cosets, or the guard connecting-map argument, prove the
reverse inclusion.  Condition (i) does not depend on \(\lambda\): the
weighted boundary space is \(W_{d_a}^{-1}B_{d_a}(Y_a;1)\) and \(W_{d_a}\)
is the identity on register coordinates.  Modular rank bounds with matching
rational kernels certify (ii), and a nonzero Gram determinant certifies
(iii).  The subspace \(Q_a\) need not be spanned by simplex coordinates; the
selected-cycle guards of \cref{app:palette} produce \(Q_a\) of the form
\(Q\otimes\C\gamma\).  The explicit palette used later is recorded in
\cref{app:palette}.

\begin{lemma}[Harmonic padding]
\label{lem:scaling-padding}
Let \(0<\lambda\le1\).  Let \(Y_a\) be joined with the unaffected register
factors, whose join \(R_{\rm out}\) has reduced homology only in its top
degree, with top harmonic space \(\cH_{\rm out}\), and let \(d\) be the
global target degree.  Then the zero-weight kernel of the padded gadget in
degree \(d\) is
\[
V\oplus\bigl(Q_a\otimes\cH_{\rm out}\bigr),
\]
its orthogonal complement has a uniform positive zero-weight floor, the
projected pair \(T_a(\lambda)\otimes P_{\cH_{\rm out}}\) satisfies (iii)
with the same constant \(b\), and the padded exact boundary intersection
with \(V\) is the range of
\(\Pi_a=\pi_a\otimes I_{\cH_{\rm out}}\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
See \cref{app:local-padding}.
\end{proof}

The harmonic restriction in this lemma is essential.  If the padded bulk
contained every coordinate with a local private vertex, deleting a weight-one
outside vertex could remain in that bulk with coefficient one.  We instead
keep local private coordinates tensored with outside harmonics and place every
nonharmonic outside factor in the separately gapped sector.

\begin{definition}[Filling depth]
\label{def:depth}
Let \(c_a\in C_{d_a+1}(Y_a;\mathbb Z)\) be the certified integer filling of
the integer register cycle \(v_a\) spanning \(\ran\pi_a\), so that
\(\partial c_a=v_a\) in unweighted coordinates.  Its \emph{depth} \(p_a\) is
the largest number of private vertices in a simplex of the support of
\(c_a\), and the depth of the palette is \(p=\max_ap_a\ge1\).
\end{definition}
The depth is a combinatorial invariant of the certificate and is read off
from the filling data; \cref{app:palette} lists it for every member.  It
controls the only power of \(\lambda\) in the theorem: in weighted
coordinates the filling becomes \(W_{d_a+1}^{-1}c_a\), whose norm is at most
\(\|c_a\|\lambda^{-p_a}\).

\subsection{Global theorem}

Attach \(t_A\) padded gadgets to the common top-dimensional register \(R\)
with independent private interiors (\cref{def:independent}).  Let \(L_j\) be
the block of \(\partial_{X_A,d}\) that maps the private \(d\)-chains of
gadget \(j\) to the register \((d-1)\)-chains.  A private \(d\)-simplex has a
register face only if it contains exactly one private vertex, and removing
that vertex gives coefficient \(\pm\lambda\); hence every column of
\(\lambda^{-1}L_j\) has at most one nonzero entry and every row has at most
\(p_j\) nonzero entries, where \(p_j\) is the number of private vertices of
gadget \(j\).  Consequently the finite interface bound
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:interface}
L_jL_j^*\preceq N_j\lambda^2I,\qquad N_j\le N_*,
\end{equation}
holds with \(N_j\le p_j\), so \(N_*\) is a palette constant.
The input blocks are orthogonal, while the outputs may overlap.  Hence, for
\(L=[L_1\ \cdots\ L_{t_A}]\),
\[
LL^*=\sum_jL_jL_j^*\preceq N_*t_A\lambda^2I.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Finite-certificate degenerate-kernel transfer]
\label{thm:transfer}
Fix a finite palette satisfying \cref{ass:local-certificate} with depth
\(p\), and let \(\kappa=2p\).  Let
\(\mathcal A\) be a finite family of term sets whose gadgets are padded
palette members attached to the register \(R\) of \cref{sec:prelim} with
independent private interiors, all with the same private weight \(\lambda\).
Let \(t_{\max}=\max\{1,\max_{A\in\mathcal A}|A|\}\) and suppose
\[
H_A=\sum_{j\in A}\Pi_j\succeq
g_A(P_V-P_{K_A}),\qquad K_A=\ker H_A,\qquad 0<g_A\le1
\]
for every \(A\in\mathcal A\).
There are palette constants \(c_0,C>0\) such that, whenever
\(0<\lambda\le\min\{1,c_0/t_{\max}\}\), every unit \(x\in C_d(X_A)\)
satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:transfer-bound}
\boxed{\;
\|(I-P_{K_A})x\|^2
\le C\left[t_A\lambda^2+
\frac{\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle}{g_A\lambda^\kappa}\right].\;}
\end{equation}
Here \(P_{K_A}\) is the projector onto the embedded logical kernel, extended
by zero outside the register.

For \(0<\eta<1\), choose additionally
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda-E-choice}
\lambda\le c_1\min\{t_{\max}^{-1},\eta/\sqrt{t_{\max}}\},
\qquad E_A\le c_2\eta^2g_A\lambda^\kappa,
\end{equation}
with palette constants \(c_1,c_2>0\).  Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:kernel-gap-conclusion}
\dim\ker\Delta_A=\dim K_A,\qquad
\spec(\Delta_A)\cap(0,E_A)=\varnothing .
\end{equation}
This includes \(K_A=0\), in which case \(\Delta_A\succeq E_AI\).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Proof architecture.}
The full calculation is in \cref{app:transfer-proof}.  The shared-output
identity above and the private projected pairs first give the geometric
leakage estimate
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:geometric-leakage}
\|(I-P_V)x\|^2
\le C\left[t_A\lambda^2+
(t_A+\lambda^{-2})\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle\right].
\end{equation}
The certified fillings give, by a Schur-complement argument
(\cref{lem:filling-domination}),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:logical-domination}
\Delta_A^\uparrow\succeq c\lambda^{2p} H_A^{\rm emb},
\end{equation}
where \(\Delta_A^\uparrow=\partial_{d+1}\partial_{d+1}^*\) and
\(H_A^{\rm emb}\) is \(H_A\) extended by zero outside \(V\).  This is the only
step in which a power of \(\lambda\) beyond \(\lambda^2\) appears, which is
why the exponent depends on the fillings alone and not on the locality of
the gadgets.
Combining these inequalities with the logical gap proves
\cref{eq:transfer-bound}.  The bound applies to every vector in a low-energy
spectral subspace.  Projection onto \(K_A\) is therefore injective on that
subspace, so its dimension is at most \(\dim K_A\).  Independent exact
fillings supply \(\dim K_A\) zero modes through \(V/W_A\); equality and the
whole-positive-spectrum gap follow.  No relative-acyclicity assumption is
used.

For the explicit history palette \(p=5\) (\cref{app:palette}), so
\(\kappa=10\).  Taking a dyadic \(\lambda=\Theta(\eta/t_{\max})\) gives the
conservative scale
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:weighted-gap}
E_A=\Omega\!\left(\frac{\eta^{12}g_A}{t_{\max}^{10}}\right).
\end{equation}
For the certified atoms the bound \(\Delta^\uparrow\succeq c\lambda^{2p_a}\Pi\)
is tight up to the constant (the optimal constant scales exactly as
\(\lambda^{2p_a}\) numerically), so a better exponent would need gadgets with
shallower fillings.
