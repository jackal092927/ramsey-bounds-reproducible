For an inclusion of clique complexes \(X_1\subseteq X_2\), the normalized persistence in degree \(d\) is the fraction
\[
\frac{\rank[H_d(X_1)\to H_d(X_2)]}{\dim H_d(X_1)}
\]
of the degree-\(d\) holes of \(X_1\) that survive in \(X_2\). Lowe, Kim, Bondesan, and Hayakawa asked for the complexity of estimating this fraction under inverse-polynomial spectral-gap promises on both endpoint Laplacians, conjectured hardness for a variant of \(\mathsf{DQC}_1\) with perfect completeness, and reduced the conjecture to an open kernel-preserving realization of history Hamiltonians by clique complexes.
We prove that estimating the fraction to additive error \(1/24\) is hard for \(\BQPone^{G_2}\), the perfect-completeness class over the exact gate set \(G_2=\{X,\CX,\CCX,H\otimes H\}\), and hence for \(\BQPone\) over every finite gate set with entries in a cyclotomic field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2^k})\), even for unweighted clique complexes.
The main tool is a finite-certificate kernel-transfer theorem. For a fixed palette of weighted clique gadgets satisfying finitely many exactly checkable local conditions, every unit chain \(x\) of the full geometric complex satisfies
\[
\dist(x,K)^2\le C\Bigl(t\lambda^2+\frac{\langle x,\Delta x\rangle}{g\lambda^{26}}\Bigr),
\]
where \(K\) is the embedded kernel of the simulated projector Hamiltonian, \(g\) its gap, \(t\) the number of gadgets, and \(\lambda\) the private vertex weight. Since \(\lambda\) is chosen independently of \(g\), the geometric Laplacian has exactly \(\dim K\) zero modes and a gap linear in \(g\) above them. Exact fillings identify the endpoint homology with a quotient \(V/W_A\) of the register cycle space, and nested term sets induce the natural quotient epimorphisms, so the persistent rank equals the later kernel dimension without any choice of compatible harmonic representatives. A fixed eight-dimensional label register turns a \(\BQPone^{G_2}\) verifier into instances with \(\beta_d(X_1)=8\) and normalized persistence \(3/4\) or \(1/8\), and Hayakawa's common-copy blow-up transfers everything to unweighted graphs.
