\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}

The result has four material boundaries. First, the transfer theorem is instantiated by a fixed finite real-gate palette. The local hypotheses are exact and checkable, but the construction does not cover arbitrary integer states or complex phases. Second, the complexity conclusion is gate dependent. It applies to \(\BQPone^{G_2}\) and to the fixed cyclotomic gate families covered by an exact simulation theorem; it does not establish ordinary \(\BQP\), unrestricted \(\SDQCone\), or a gate-independent \(\BQPone\) result.

Third, the cleanest theorem has the fixed denominator \(\beta_d(\Xin)=8\). This is a strong structural restriction, but it also means that hardness is carried by deciding which of eight logical classes survive rather than by estimating a density over an intrinsically growing family of classes. Tensoring ignored mixed qubits makes the denominator grow while preserving the ratio, but that operation only replicates the same eight-label structure. An intrinsic growing-denominator theorem needs a different exact source whose perfect eigenspace fraction is hard without ancillary replication.

Fourth, the gap bound is designed for polynomial separation rather than numerical use. At locality six, the weighted estimate scales as \(\Omega(\eta^{28}g/t^{26})\), and the history gap contributes \(g=\Omega(L^{-3})\). The unweighted blow-up remains polynomial but can be large. These bounds support a complexity reduction and do not imply a near-term algorithmic advantage.

There is also a precise priority boundary. The qualitative degenerate-kernel closure is a short consequence of any valid King--Kohler-style arbitrary-chain master estimate. The present proof contribution is the finite-certificate replacement for two nonuniform padded steps and the resulting logical-gap-independent choice of \(\lambda\). Our comparison has checked arXiv version 2, while the detailed final SIAM text was unavailable during this draft. If that version already contains an equivalent fixed-local outside-harmonic repair with a quadratic \(t\lambda^2\) remainder, the analytic novelty should be reduced accordingly. The theorem and its normalized-persistence application remain mathematically meaningful, but the paper's positioning would shift toward synthesis and application.

Within these boundaries, the construction gives a complete route from degenerate logical kernels to true normalized persistence. A finite collection of local certificates controls every geometric chain. Exact filling supplies the intended zero modes, concentration rules out extras and opens a gap above the complete kernel, and quotient naturality turns endpoint control into an exact persistent rank. The fixed-eight source then yields separated normalized values, and a common-copy corollary removes the weights. The reusable output is the transfer criterion: future reductions can obtain gapped persistent multiplicities by verifying finite local zero-weight, private-coercivity, padding, filling, and interface conditions rather than repeating a growing-dimensional perturbation analysis.
