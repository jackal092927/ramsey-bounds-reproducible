\section{Finite-certificate transfer to the complete geometric kernel}
\label{sec:transfer}

We isolate finite local conditions under which a clique-gadget construction
controls every chain in the full geometric complex.  The statement does not
use convergence of a spectral projector whose rank grows with the simulated
circuit.

\subsection{The certificate model}

Consider one active gadget before padding.  Its weight-one register is \(R_a\),
its target cycle space is \(V_a=Z_{d_a}(R_a)\), and every private vertex has
weight \(\lambda\).  In normalized chain coordinates put
\[
D_a(\lambda)=
\begin{bmatrix}\partial_{d_a}(\lambda)\\
\partial_{d_a+1}(\lambda)^*\end{bmatrix}
=D_{a,0}+\lambda D_{a,1}.
\]
The value at \(\lambda=0\) is a matrix limit, not a singular change of basis.

\begin{assumption}[Finite local certificate]
\label{ass:local-certificate}
For every member \(a\) of a fixed finite palette there are an orthogonal
subspace \(Q_a\) contained in the span of private simplex coordinates, an
output projector \(R_a^{\rm out}\), and a rank-one
projector \(\pi_a\) on \(V_a\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item \(B_{d_a}(Y_a)\cap V_a=\ran\pi_a\) for every \(\lambda>0\);
\item \(\ker D_{a,0}=V_a\oplus Q_a\), orthogonally;
\item for \(T_a(\lambda)=R_a^{\rm out}D_a(\lambda)\),
\[
T_a(\lambda)=\lambda T_{a,1},\qquad T_{a,1}V_a=0,\qquad
\|T_{a,1}q\|\ge b\|q\|\quad(q\in Q_a);
\]
\item every retained output coordinate contains a private vertex.
\end{enumerate}
The constant \(b>0\), the positive singular-value floor of \(D_{a,0}\), and
all matrix norms are uniform over the palette.
\end{assumption}

All data in \cref{ass:local-certificate} are finite integer or rational linear
algebra.  Exact fillings prove one inclusion in (i); matching boundary ranks
and surviving register cosets, or the guard connecting-map argument, prove the
reverse inclusion.  Modular rank bounds with matching rational kernels certify
(ii), and a nonzero Gram determinant certifies (iii).
The explicit palette used later is recorded in \cref{app:palette}.

\begin{lemma}[Weight scaling and harmonic padding]
\label{lem:scaling-padding}
Suppose \(d_a=2m_a-1\) and \(m_a\le m\).  There is a palette constant \(c>0\)
such that
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:local-gap}
\gamma_+\!\left(\Delta_{Y_a,d_a}(\lambda)\right)
\ge c\lambda^\kappa,\qquad \kappa:=4m+2.
\end{equation}
If \(Y_a\) is joined with unaffected register factors whose reduced homology
is concentrated in top degree, then in the global target degree the
zero-weight kernel is
\[
V\oplus\bigl(Q_a\otimes\cH_{\rm out}\bigr),
\]
its orthogonal complement has a uniform positive floor, and the padded exact
boundary intersection is the range of
\(\Pi_a=\pi_a\otimes I_{\cH_{\rm out}}\) on \(V\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
See \cref{app:local-padding}.
\end{proof}

The harmonic restriction in this lemma is essential.  If the padded bulk
contained every coordinate with a local private vertex, deleting a weight-one
outside vertex could remain in that bulk with coefficient one.  We instead
keep local private coordinates tensored with outside harmonics and place every
nonharmonic outside factor in the separately gapped sector.

\subsection{Global theorem}

Attach \(t_A\) padded gadgets to the common top-dimensional register \(R\).
Their private interiors are disjoint.  Let \(L_j\) map the private input block
of gadget \(j\) to the shared register output and assume the finite interface
bound
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:interface}
L_jL_j^*\preceq N_j\lambda^2I,\qquad N_j\le N_*.
\end{equation}
The input blocks are orthogonal, while the outputs may overlap.  Hence, for
\(L=[L_1\ \cdots\ L_{t_A}]\),
\[
LL^*=\sum_jL_jL_j^*\preceq N_*t_A\lambda^2I.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Finite-certificate degenerate-kernel transfer]
\label{thm:transfer}
Fix a palette satisfying \cref{ass:local-certificate,lem:scaling-padding} and
\cref{eq:interface}.  Use outside-harmonic padding in every bidegree and
independent private interiors.  Let \(\mathcal A\) be a finite nested family
of nonempty term sets, let
\(t_{\max}=\max_{A\in\mathcal A}|A|\), and suppose
\[
H_A=\sum_{j\in A}\Pi_j\succeq
g_A(P_V-P_{K_A}),\qquad K_A=\ker H_A,\qquad 0<g_A\le1
\]
for every \(A\in\mathcal A\).
There are palette constants \(c_0,C>0\) such that, whenever
\(0<\lambda\le c_0/t_{\max}\), every unit \(x\in C_d(X_A)\) satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:transfer-bound}
\boxed{\;
\|(I-P_{K_A})x\|^2
\le C\left[t_A\lambda^2+
\frac{\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle}{g_A\lambda^\kappa}\right].\;}
\end{equation}
Here \(P_{K_A}\) is the projector onto the embedded logical kernel, extended
by zero outside the register.

For \(0<\eta<1\), choose additionally
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda-E-choice}
\lambda\le c_1\min\{t_{\max}^{-1},\eta/\sqrt{t_{\max}}\},
\qquad E_A\le c_2\eta^2g_A\lambda^\kappa.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:kernel-gap-conclusion}
\dim\ker\Delta_A=\dim K_A,\qquad
\spec(\Delta_A)\cap(0,E_A)=\varnothing .
\end{equation}
This includes \(K_A=0\).
\end{theorem}

The bare-register level \(A=\varnothing\), if included in a filtration, is
handled separately: \(X_A=R\), \(K_A=V\), and the tensor-join bowtie register
has a constant positive degree-\(d\) gap on \(V^\perp\).  Thus it may be
adjoined without invoking the many-gadget estimate, whose proof assumes
\(t_A\ge1\).

\paragraph{Proof architecture.}
The full calculation is in \cref{app:transfer-proof}.  The shared-output
identity above and the private projected pairs first give the geometric
leakage estimate
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:geometric-leakage}
\|(I-P_V)x\|^2
\le C\left[t_A\lambda^2+
(t_A+\lambda^{-2})\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle\right].
\end{equation}
Exact filling and \cref{lem:scaling-padding} separately give
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:logical-domination}
\Delta_A^\uparrow\succeq c\lambda^\kappa H_A^{\rm emb}.
\end{equation}
Combining these inequalities with the logical gap proves
\cref{eq:transfer-bound}.  The bound applies to every vector in a low-energy
spectral subspace.  Projection onto \(K_A\) is therefore injective on that
subspace, so its dimension is at most \(\dim K_A\).  Independent exact
fillings supply \(\dim K_A\) zero modes through \(V/W_A\); equality and the
whole-positive-spectrum gap follow.  No relative-acyclicity assumption is
used.

For the explicit history palette \(m\le6\), so \(\kappa=26\).  Taking a
dyadic \(\lambda=\Theta(\eta/t_{\max})\) gives the conservative scale
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:weighted-gap}
E_A=\Omega\!\left(\frac{\eta^{28}g_A}{t_{\max}^{26}}\right).
\end{equation}
The exponent is not claimed to be optimal.
