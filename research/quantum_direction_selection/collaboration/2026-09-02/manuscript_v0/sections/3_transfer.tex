\section{Finite-certificate transfer to the complete geometric kernel}
\label{sec:transfer}

We state the local hypotheses in a form that can be certified once for a fixed gadget palette. The proof never appeals to convergence of a spectral projector in a chain space whose dimension grows with the simulated circuit.

\subsection{Local certificates}

Consider one active gadget before padding. Let \(R_a\) be its weight-one active register, let \(V_a=Z_{d_a}(R_a)\), and give each new vertex weight \(\lambda\). In normalized chain coordinates define the degree-\(d_a\) differential pair
\[
D_a(\lambda)=
\begin{bmatrix}
\partial_{d_a}(\lambda)\\
\partial_{d_a+1}(\lambda)^*
\end{bmatrix}
=D_{a,0}+\lambda D_{a,1}.
\]
The value at \(\lambda=0\) is a matrix limit; no singular change of basis at zero is used.

\begin{assumption}[Finite local certificate]
\label{ass:local-certificate}
For every member \(a\) of a fixed finite palette, there is a private-coordinate subspace \(Q_a\), an output projector \(R_a^{\rm out}\), and a rank-one projector \(\Pi_a\) on \(V_a\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item \(B_{d_a}(Y_a)\cap V_a=\ran\Pi_a\) for every positive \(\lambda\);
\item \(\ker D_{a,0}=V_a\oplus Q_a\), orthogonally;
\item with \(T_a(\lambda)=R_a^{\rm out}D_a(\lambda)\),
\[
T_a(\lambda)=\lambda T_{a,1},
\qquad T_{a,1}V_a=0,
\qquad \|T_{a,1}q\|\ge b\|q\|
\quad(q\in Q_a);
\]
\item all retained output coordinates in \(R_a^{\rm out}\) contain a private vertex of the gadget.
\end{enumerate}
The constant \(b>0\), all matrix norms, and the positive spectral floors at \(\lambda=1\) are uniform over the palette.
\end{assumption}

These conditions are finite linear algebra. Exact filling chains certify (i). Kernel vectors and modular rank certificates certify (ii). A Gram determinant or rational singular-value lower bound certifies (iii). The private support in (iv) prevents cancellation between different gadgets.

The elementary diagonal weight scaling gives a coarse local gap. If all active degrees satisfy \(d_a=2m_a-1\) with \(m_a\le m\), then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:local-gap}
\gamma_+\bigl(\Delta_{Y_a,d_a}(\lambda)\bigr)
\ge c\lambda^{4m+2}
=c\lambda^\kappa,
\qquad \kappa:=4m+2.
\end{equation}
Indeed, writing \(W_k\) for the diagonal matrix of simplex weights gives
\(
\partial_{w,k}=W_{k-1}^{-1}\partial_{1,k}W_k
\).
Since \(\sigma_{\min}(W_k)\ge\lambda^{k+1}\), every nonzero singular value is bounded below by a fixed unweighted singular value times \(\lambda^{k+1}\). The finite palette turns the remaining constants into a common \(c>0\).

\subsection{Padding and interfaces}

An active gadget is joined with the unused register factors. We use reduced chain complexes, including degree \(-1\), and decompose the target chain degree over all join bidegrees. The unused register has reduced homology only in its top degree and a constant positive gap elsewhere. The join identity
\[
\Delta_{Y_a*R_{\rm out}}
=\Delta_{Y_a}\otimes I+I\otimes\Delta_{R_{\rm out}}
\]
then shows that the padded zero-weight kernel is precisely
\[
V\oplus(Q_a\otimes\cH_{\rm out}).
\]
The private output projector is tensored with the outside harmonic projector. Outside differentials vanish on this subspace, so the fixed active estimate survives padding with constants independent of the outside dimension.

This harmonic restriction is necessary. If one instead defines the padded bulk as every coordinate containing a local central vertex, deleting a weight-one outside vertex remains in that coordinate bulk with coefficient one. The corresponding norm is not \(O(\lambda)\). Thus the proof controls local bulk tensored with outside harmonics and sends every nonharmonic outside factor to the high-energy sector.

Attach \(t_A\) padded gadgets to the common register \(R\). Distinct gadgets have disjoint private chain blocks. Let \(L_j\) map the private input coordinates of gadget \(j\) to the shared register output. We assume the fixed-interface estimate
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:interface}
L_jL_j^*\preceq N_j\lambda^2 I,
\qquad N_j\le N_*.
\end{equation}
The inputs of the \(L_j\) are orthogonal, although their outputs lie in the same register. Hence for the horizontal concatenation \(L=[L_1\ \cdots\ L_{t_A}]\),
\[
LL^*=\sum_jL_jL_j^*
\preceq N_*t_A\lambda^2I.
\]
No output-orthogonality assumption is present here. Orthogonality is used later only for the retained private maps \(T_j\).

\subsection{All-chain theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-certificate degenerate-kernel transfer]
\label{thm:transfer}
Fix a palette satisfying~\cref{ass:local-certificate,eq:local-gap,eq:interface}, use outside-harmonic padding in every bidegree, and attach gadgets with independent private interiors. Let \(\mathcal A\) be any finite nested family of term sets, \(t_{\max}=\max_{A\in\mathcal A}|A|\), and suppose~\cref{eq:logical-gap} holds for every \(A\in\mathcal A\).

There are palette constants \(c_0,C>0\) such that, whenever
\[
0<\lambda\le c_0/t_{\max},
\]
every unit chain \(x\in C_d(X_A)\) satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:transfer-bound}
\boxed{
\|(I-P_{K_A})x\|^2
\le C\left[t_A\lambda^2+
\frac{\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle}
{g_A\lambda^\kappa}\right].}
\end{equation}
Here \(P_{K_A}\) denotes the projector onto the embedded logical kernel, extended by zero on all other chains.

For every \(0<\eta<1\), choose additionally
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lambda-E-choice}
\lambda\le c_1\min\{t_{\max}^{-1},\eta/\sqrt{t_{\max}}\},
\qquad
E_A\le c_2\eta^2g_A\lambda^\kappa.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:kernel-gap-conclusion}
\dim\ker\Delta_A=\dim K_A,
\qquad
\spec(\Delta_A)\cap(0,E_A)=\varnothing.
\end{equation}
The conclusion includes \(K_A=0\).
\end{theorem}

The proof has two independent parts. First, zero-weight coercivity, the private projected pair, and interface absorption give the purely geometric leakage estimate
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:geometric-leakage}
\|(I-P_V)x\|^2
\le C\left[t_A\lambda^2+
(t_A+\lambda^{-2})\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle\right].
\end{equation}
Second, exact filling and the local positive floor imply
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:logical-domination}
\Delta_A^\uparrow\succeq
c\lambda^\kappa H_A^{\rm emb}.
\end{equation}
To justify~\cref{eq:logical-domination}, note explicitly that \(\ran\Pi_j\subseteq B_d(Y_j)\), boundary chains are orthogonal to the local harmonic kernel, and the up-Laplacian has gap at least~\cref{eq:local-gap} on its range. Since \(R\) is top-dimensional and private interiors are independent, all degree-\((d+1)\) columns partition by gadget. Summing the local inequalities gives~\cref{eq:logical-domination}.

Combining~\cref{eq:logical-gap,eq:geometric-leakage,eq:logical-domination} proves~\cref{eq:transfer-bound}. The full calculation appears in \cref{app:transfer-proof}. The estimate is uniform over every geometric chain, so it applies to every vector in a low-energy spectral subspace, including arbitrary linear combinations of eigenvectors.

\begin{remark}[Relation to the positive-definite argument]
Suppose one already had an arbitrary-chain inequality
\[
1-\langle x,P_Vx\rangle+\langle x,H_A^{\rm emb}x\rangle\le R(e),
\qquad e=\langle x,\Delta_Ax\rangle.
\]
Then~\cref{eq:logical-gap} immediately gives
\[
\|(I-P_{K_A})x\|^2\le R(e)/g_A.
\]
Thus the substitution of a degenerate logical gap and the later min--max closure are elementary once a correct uniform all-chain estimate exists. The contribution of \cref{thm:transfer} is the finite-certificate proof of that estimate, including the padded-sector repair and the independence of \(\lambda\) from \(g_A\).
\end{remark}

For \(m\le6\) in the explicit history palette, \(\kappa=26\). Taking \(\lambda=\Theta(\eta/t_{\max})\) in~\cref{eq:lambda-E-choice} yields the conservative scale
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:weighted-gap}
E_A=\Omega\!\left(\frac{\eta^{28}g_A}{t_{\max}^{26}}\right).
\end{equation}
The theorem uses only a polynomial lower bound; no claim of an optimal exponent is intended.
