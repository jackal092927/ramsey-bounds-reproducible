## Lemma 1 — PSD coefficient erasure + minmax transfer

**Verdict: partially supported. Two steps are fine, one step is near-vacuous, and the persistence claim is false without an extra hypothesis.**

**(a) Boundary decomposition — supported, conditionally.** Let $c\in C_{d+1}(X_A)$ with $\partial c\in V$. Since $R$ carries no $(d+1)$-cells and gadget interiors are pairwise disjoint, $c=\sum_j c_j$ with $c_j$ supported in $Y_j$. Off-register components of $\partial c_j$ cannot cancel against $\partial c_k$ ($j\neq k$), so each $\partial c_j$ is supported in $R$; being a cycle, it lies in $Z_d(R)=V$, hence in $B_d(Y_j)\cap V=\operatorname{ran}P_j$. So $B_d(X_A)\cap V=\sum_j\operatorname{ran}P_j$. ✓ **But this needs stated explicitly:** (i) $V$ is the *full* cycle space $Z_d(R)$, not a proper subspace (otherwise $\partial c_j\in Z_d(R)\setminus V$ is possible and the sum only contains $\Sigma\operatorname{ran}P_j$); (ii) no gadget contributes a $(d+1)$-cell supported entirely in $R$; (iii) field coefficients. Over $\mathbb Z$ the quotient $V/\sum\operatorname{ran}P_j$ can acquire torsion and the injection-into-$H_d$/zero-mode-count correspondence breaks (Hodge theory needs $\mathbb R$).

**(b) Minmax transfer — logically valid but circular.** The argument is correct: for unit $x$ in the spectral subspace $S=\operatorname{ran}\mathbb 1_{[0,E)}(H_A)$ the Rayleigh quotient is $<E$, so $\|\Pi x\|\ge\sqrt{1-\eta^2}>0$, $\Pi|_S$ injective, $\dim S\le r_A$, and since $\ker H_A\subseteq S$ you get $S=\ker H_A$ and gap $\ge E$. ✓ **However the hypothesis already *is* the conclusion.** If $x\perp\ker H_A$ is unit with energy $<E$, the hypothesis gives $1=\|(I-\Pi)x\|\le\eta<1$ — contradiction, in one line, with no spectral projector needed. Diagnostic smell: $\eta$ never appears in the conclusion. A genuine erasure lemma should degrade, e.g. gap $\ge E(1-\eta^2)$ under a hypothesis restricted to a proper class of $x$. If your real hypothesis holds only for $x$ in some subspace (e.g. only for register-supported $x$, or with $\eta$ depending on $E$), the injection fails and nothing survives. Also: $E$ is asserted uniformly in $A$; for $H_A=\sum_{j\in A}P_j$ with frustrated projectors $\lambda_{\min}^{\neq0}$ typically decays as $1/\mathrm{poly}(|A|)$, so a $|A|$-independent $E$ is a substantive unproved claim.

**(c) "Register quotient is now an isomorphism" — not supported; counterexample.** You proved *injectivity* $V/W_A\hookrightarrow H_d(X_A)$. Surjectivity requires every $d$-cycle of $X_A$ to be homologous to a register cycle. **Counterexample ($d=1$):** $R$ a triangulated circle, $V=Z_1(R)$; $Y_1$ a disk filling $R$ ($P_1=I$, $r_A=0$); $Y_2$ a hollow torus glued to $R$ along a curve. Then $V/W_A=0$ but $H_1(X_A)\neq0$, so the Laplacian on $C_d(X_A)$ has zero modes not counted by $r_A$ — "exactly $r_A$ zeros and gap $\ge E$" is false for the geometric Hamiltonian even though it is true for $H_A=\sum P_j$ acting on $V$. Note the lemma silently switches between these two operators. The nested surjection $V/W_A\twoheadrightarrow V/W_B$ is correct and natural, but "persistence ratio $=\dim\ker H_B/\dim\ker H_A$" is then a tautology about $V$ ($\dim\ker H_A=\dim V-\dim W_A$), not a statement about the persistence of $H_d(X_A)\to H_d(X_B)$.

**Strongest missing assumption (L1):** each gadget is *relatively $d$-acyclic* over the register — $Y_j$ contributes no $d$-cycle not homologous into $R$ (e.g. $H_d(Y_j,Y_j\cap R)$ vanishing in the relevant degree). Without it, injection ≠ isomorphism and the persistence ratio is wrong.

## Lemma 2 — rational weighted history

**Verdict: supported as arithmetic; scope disclaimer is honest; one structural hypothesis is missing and one constant is unearned.**

Checks that pass: $A_2A_1=(I\otimes H/\sqrt2)(\sqrt2H\otimes I)=H\otimes H$ ✓. $A_1A_1^\dagger=2I$ so $BB^\dagger=(2I+I)/3=I$ ✓; $A_2^{-1}=\sqrt2H$, $A_2^{-1}A_2^{-\dagger}=2I$, again $BB^\dagger=I$ ✓ — both edge terms are genuine rank-$n$ orthogonal projectors with $\ker B$ the intended constraint. Rationality is real: $\sqrt2H=\begin{psmallmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{psmallmatrix}$, so both $B^\dagger B$ have all entries in $\tfrac13\mathbb Z$ ✓. Edge energies: $\tfrac13\|A_1\psi_{t-1}-\psi_t\|^2=\tfrac13\cdot2\|\xi_t-\xi_{t-1}\|^2=\tfrac23\|\Delta\xi\|^2$, and identically for the $A_2$ edge ✓; $\tfrac23>\tfrac12$, so the form dominates the standard path Laplacian ✓. $\|\Psi\|^2=\sum s_t^2\|\xi_t\|^2\in[\sum\|\xi_t\|^2,2\sum\|\xi_t\|^2]$ ✓. Induced $Q$ on the kernel: $\langle\Psi|Q|\Psi\rangle=\langle\xi_0|(I-M)|\xi_0\rangle$, $\|\Psi\|^2=Z\|\xi_0\|^2$, so $(I-M)/Z$ ✓, and $\alpha\ge(2/3)/2L=1/(3L)$ ✓. Final arithmetic $\alpha g_1/5=1/(120L^3)$ ✓ internally consistent.

**Gap 1 (fixable, costs a constant).** Orthogonality to the kernel is in the *weighted* inner product ($\sum s_t^2\xi_t=0$), not $\sum\xi_t=0$. The unweighted path-Poincaré inequality does not apply verbatim; you need either the weighted-measure Poincaré or the bound $\|\bar\xi\|\le\sqrt{k}/(L{+}1)\,\|\xi\|$ ($k=\#$ inflated times). Costs $\le$ factor 2, but must be written.

**Gap 2 (serious).** $g_1=1/(8L^2)$ is claimed for the kernel of $H_{\rm prop}+H_{\rm in}$. Path Poincaré gives $\Omega(1/L^2)$ for $H_{\rm prop}$ alone; "anchoring" is a second geometric-lemma application, and the naive route ($H_{\rm in}$ induces $\Pi^\perp/Z$, gap $1/Z\approx1/2L$, NPL) yields only $\Omega(1/L^3)$, hence $1/L^4$ overall. Kitaev's $\Omega(1/L^2)$ for prop+input is a genuine computation in a rotated basis and must be redone with $s_t\in\{1,\sqrt2\}$; the constant $1/8$ is not derived.

**Strongest missing assumption (L2): the gain/loss pair must be strictly consecutive and never nested, interleaved, or stacked.** If two $A_1$-type steps occur before their matching $A_2$ steps, $s_t=2,2\sqrt2,\dots$ grows geometrically, $Z$ blows up exponentially, and $\alpha\sim1/Z$ collapses — killing the whole bound. Also require: every $A_i$ invertible (✓ here) so the kernel is exactly one history per input; $s_L=1$, i.e. the circuit never terminates mid-pair, else $U$ is non-unitary and $M$ is not a valid acceptance operator; $\|Q\|\le1$ and $\ker H_1\cap\ker Q\neq0$ for the $x=p+q$ step. Finally, "rank ratio" is a dimension count $\dim(\text{perfect-accept}\cap\text{clean-input})/\dim(\text{input})$, not a rank ratio — worth renaming to avoid importing Lemma 1's persistence language.
