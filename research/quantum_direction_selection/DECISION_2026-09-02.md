## 1. Executive decision

The best concrete next mathematical target is **filtration-compatible unweighting of quantum harmonic persistence**. This remains within quantum TDA, but it is not a quantization of the full Dey–Xin/AIDA decomposition algorithm. The exact commutation and conditional harmonic-transfer argument work. The actual two-weight source hard family appears compatible, including its compact initial guide. **The final assessment is a likely direct corollary of very recent work, not yet a sufficiently novel new algorithm.** Finish its source/parameter verification before investing in a larger paper.

The closest direct quantization of AIDA is less ready: replacing one candidate splitting direction can cause a linearized consistency update of arbitrarily large rank, even inside a valid minimal presentation with an indecomposable target block. The classical algebraic baseline also needs to be respected. Do not build a quantum implementation before a better algorithmic lemma exists.

For hyperbolic navigation, stop the specific proposal “apply Grover to the neighbor list in fixed dimension.” That does not stop all quantum hyperbolic algorithms. A growing-dimensional structured-filter/query-space result remains a backup, but currently has neither a new geometric sampling lemma nor a verified end-to-end advantage.

**No publishable new quantum speedup has been established in this exploration.** We have concrete structural results and a narrower positive target, rather than only a list of speculative titles.

## 4. Results obtained and independently checked

### A. A real simplicial example defeats the raw-projector conditioning inference

**LOCAL DERIVATION, analytic proof and exact local fixture.** A chain of fixed triangulated Möbius bands gives a genuine simplicial-inclusion zigzag, not merely an arbitrary matrix example. Each object is constant size and the two module constraint gaps remain bounded below. Its generalized rank is always one, while the nonzero overlap of the two relevant kernel subspaces is Theta(m 2^-m).

The root checked the kernel/norm and block-symbol argument and reran the exact rational and numerical fixture. At m=32 the overlap is about 6.10e-9 despite D/B gaps above 0.218. Hence local good conditioning does not justify a polynomial-cost raw-projector rank estimator.

This is useful because it rules out a specific attractive approach on authentic topological input. It is **not** a lower bound against every quantum algorithm. Möbius/telescope mechanisms have prior literature, so publication novelty is unproved. See [complete proof](tda_probe/PROBE_REPORT.md).

### B. Hyperbolic geometry gives a stronger classical distance-query baseline

**LOCAL DERIVATION, elementary proof and numerical check.** After query-independent geometric preprocessing, distances from a query to at most d+1 landmark data points determine all its distance scores on any finite set in H^d. This follows from the Lorentz bilinear expression for cosh distance. A maximum-volume anchor basis also gives a bounded-error statement for additive cosh-score noise.

The root checked the span/nondegeneracy and determinant arguments and independently reran H2/H3/H5 fixtures. This defeats the claim that classical selection necessarily needs one exact distance query per neighbor. It does not eliminate the arithmetic cost of reconstructing/scanning scores and does not prove constant-time ANN. The idea is not claimed new distance geometry. See [complete proof and one-step shell analysis](hyperbolic_probe/PROOF_PACKAGE.md).

### C. A positive conditional transfer corollary for persistence-compatible unweighting

**LOCAL DERIVATION using the cited symmetric-reduction and asymmetric-gap theorems; not a new quantum algorithm.** Suppose all filtration levels use the same positive weight on each shared vertex, and integer multiplicities satisfy f_v=M w(v)^2 for a common M. Replacing each vertex by f_v clique copies admits normalized symmetric-sector embeddings U_i. The argument gives

    J_expanded U_1 = U_2 J_weighted,
    Delta_expanded U_i = M U_i Delta_weighted.

There is no extra inclusion normalization factor. The source's asymmetric-sector theorem applies at every level and excludes additional harmonic kernels. Thus the harmonic maps are unitarily equivalent, their singular values and guided overlaps are preserved, and the new nonzero spectral gap is at least min(M gamma_i, min_v f_v).

For the June source's actual hard family, the initial guide is supported entirely on weight-one register vertices, and the added vertices have a common small weight lambda. A sufficiently small dyadic lambda and M=lambda^-2 give polynomial-size integer multiplicities. Every initial guide simplex then has the same number of copies, preserving the succinct subset-state form. The source hard family's NO overlap is exactly zero, so the graph-size change does not spoil an exponentially small NO threshold.

The root checked the identities and reran the seven-vertex fixture; its maximum residual was 7.7e-16. A second independent agent checked the source conventions, guide and threshold issue. The remaining task is a final audit of the imported theorems' exact hypotheses and the dyadic parameter substitution, not a newly identified functoriality obstacle. This is why the novelty assessment was downgraded. See [full conditional corollary](tda_probe/UNWEIGHTING_FIRST_LEMMA.md).

Limits: this does not preserve normalizations by the total number of simplices, which the blow-up changes; it does not settle the July normalized-persistence hardness conjecture; it does not compute generalized ranks efficiently. The source promise requires the final-level gap, so a claim about both levels needs a separate initial-register argument. The positive subset-state description inherits the source's simplex-orientation convention; imposing a fixed globally sorted basis can require orientation phases and changes a strictly unsigned subset-state promise. The final theorem must specify this. See the [independent corollary audit](lsh_probe/COROLLARY_AUDIT.md).

### D. AIDA's proposed cheap-update premise has a valid graded counterexample

**LOCAL DERIVATION, exact finite-field argument.** In the cached-Hom consistency system, a one-direction candidate change has update rank equal to the dimension of a Hom-evaluation image, not the rank of the changed candidate basis. This evaluation rank can be any t, even after the old relations are eliminated and the alpha-local reduction is used.

The explicit family has t+1 incomparable generator grades and one all-ones old relation. Its endomorphisms are scalar, so the target block is indecomposable, while its relevant fibre has dimension t. A map from a later free block can evaluate to any of those t directions. The root checked this reasoning and the vectorized update factorization. See [exact construction and proof](AIDA_UPDATE_TRIAGE.md). This rejects a constant-update inference, not every possible quantum decomposition algorithm.
