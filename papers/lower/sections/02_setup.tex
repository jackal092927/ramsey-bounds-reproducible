\section{Gaussian source ledger and notation}\label{sec:setup}

We use the frozen notation of the HMS/Lin--Niu bridge.  Let \(c=c(C)\) be the
central cutoff, let \(d=d(\ell,C)\) be the Gaussian dimension, and write
\[
 m(x)=\frac{\varphi(x)}{\Phi(-x)},\qquad
 m_0=m(c),\qquad m'_0=m'(c).
\tag{2.1}
\]
The scale parameter \(D=D(C)\) is chosen so that
\(d\sim D^2\ell^2\), and we put
\[
 \rho=\frac{m_0m'_0}{D},\qquad
 \omega_0=\frac{100\log(10/p_C)}D.
\tag{2.2}
\]
For fixed \(w>\omega_0\), define
\[
 M_w=m(c+w),\qquad j_-(w)=m'(c-w),\qquad j_+(w)=m'(c+w),
\tag{2.3}
\]
and
\[
 \theta=m_0^2,qquad A_0=m_0^2m'_0.
\tag{2.4}
\]
Set
\[
 a_- =\min\{A_0,\theta\},\qquad
 a_+=\max\left\{\theta,
 \frac{A_0+\rho\theta}{1-\rho}\right\},
\tag{2.5}
\]
\[
 \mu_w=j_-(w)-j_+(w)^2
 \frac{m_0+\theta/D}{D(1-\rho)},
\tag{2.6}
\]
and
\[
\widehat L_w(C)=
\frac{4m_0M_w^2}{D}
+\frac{2M_w^2a_+}{D^2}
+\frac{32m_0^2M_w^2a_+}{a_-D^2}.
\tag{2.7}
\]
Finally,
\[
 \widehat B_w(C)=m_0^2-\frac{16}{3}-\widehat L_w(C),
 \qquad
 E_w(C)=\widehat B_w(C)\theta-\frac{\theta^2}{2\mu_w},
\tag{2.8}
\]
\[
 \widehat H(C,w)=
 \frac{\widehat B_w(C)A_0+E_w(C)}{12D^2},
 \qquad
 \widehat H_*(C)=\lim_{w\downarrow\omega_0}\widehat H(C,w).
\tag{2.9}
\]

\begin{assumption}[Pinned source interface]\label{ass:hms}
We use the HMS v2 Gaussian construction, its Appendix-B reverse induction,
the red perfect-sequence extraction, and the blue comparison at the exact
locations identified in the reproducibility manifest.  We also use the
reviewed exact one-sided truncated-Gaussian CGF deficit \(G_*(C)\), inserted
before the HMS coarsening.  These inputs supply a frozen envelope
\(\mathcal H_C(r)\), a source error constant \(K\), and the unchanged
first-moment conversion from red/blue probability bounds to (1.1).
\end{assumption}

No source \(O(\cdot)\) term is assigned a numerical constant in this paper.
The construction below only deletes additional mass from the source envelope
and therefore uses the same existential \(K\).
