\section{Reproducibility and claim boundary}\label{sec:scope-lower}

The frozen pre-canonical route note, arithmetic checker, and its independent
source-level review have SHA-256 prefixes \texttt{2234a769},
\texttt{616040b9} and \texttt{ef6ddbce}, respectively.  Their historical
status labels do not override this manuscript's canonical theorem statement
or proof-status ledger.  The checker replays the recursion,
multiplicity identities, Hessian samples, exact-CGF increments, finite margins
at four large \(C\)-values and convergence to the leading constant.  It is an
diagnostic arithmetic witness, not a substitute for the reverse-induction
proof.

From the repository root run
\begin{verbatim}
bash scripts/bootstrap.sh
bash scripts/reproduce_lower.sh
\end{verbatim}
to reproduce the bridge, exact-CGF predecessor, nonexchangeable ledger and
controlled-residual arithmetic calculations with pinned dependencies.  A
passing arithmetic replay is not labelled a theorem proof.

The theorem is source-relative, fixed-\(C\), asymptotic in \(\ell\), and
non-effective in \(C_0(K)\).  It supplies no new blue-side construction and no
finite Ramsey number.  The current manuscript does not claim a common-
normalization strict improvement over the final printed Lin--Niu theorem,
global optimality, priority, publication, or world-best status.

\paragraph{Logical proof status.}
As a standalone theorem without \cref{ass:hms}, (1.1) is
\textsc{provable after weakening / extra assumption}, not an unconditional
claim.  Under the explicit items (S1)--(S5), the weighted one-step comparison,
residual contribution, extraction algebra, and crossing argument are
\textsc{provable as stated}.  In particular, the manuscript does not replace
the weighted source interface by numerical evidence.
