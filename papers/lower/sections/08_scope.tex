\section{Reproducibility and claim boundary}\label{sec:scope-lower}

The proof specification, arithmetic checker and independent source-level
review have SHA-256 prefixes \texttt{2234a769}, \texttt{616040b9} and
\texttt{ef6ddbce}, respectively.  The checker replays the recursion,
multiplicity identities, Hessian samples, exact-CGF increments, finite margins
at four large \(C\)-values and convergence to the leading constant.  It is an
arithmetic witness, not a substitute for the reverse-induction proof.

From the repository root run
\begin{verbatim}
bash scripts/bootstrap.sh
bash scripts/reproduce_lower.sh
\end{verbatim}
to reproduce the bridge, exact-CGF predecessor, nonexchangeable ledger and
controlled-residual calculations with pinned dependencies.

The theorem is source-relative, fixed-\(C\), asymptotic in \(\ell\), and
non-effective in \(C_0\).  It supplies no new blue-side construction and no
finite Ramsey number.  The current manuscript does not claim a common-
normalization strict improvement over the final printed Lin--Niu theorem,
global optimality, priority, publication, or world-best status.
