\section{Rate conversion and asymptotics}\label{sec:rate-lower}

Let \(C\to\infty\) after the fixed-\(C\) theorem and then let
\(w\downarrow\omega_0\).  The source asymptotics give
\[
 \rho=o(1),\quad m'_0\to1,\quad M_w/m_0\to1,\quad
 j_\pm(w)\to1,\quad\mu_w\to1,
\tag{7.1}
\]
\[
 \widehat L_w=o(m_0^2),\qquad
 \widehat B_w=m_0^2[1+o(1)].
\tag{7.2}
\]
Hence all feasibility and positivity conditions used above hold for
sufficiently large fixed \(C\).  Since \(K_\ell/\ell^4\to1/12\), the new
contribution to the first-moment rate is exactly (2.9).

Moreover,
\[
 m_0^2=(2+o(1))\log C,qquad
 D^2=(32+o(1))(\log C)^3,
\tag{7.3}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \widehat B_wA_0=(4+o(1))(\log C)^2,qquad
 E_w=(2+o(1))(\log C)^2.
\tag{7.4}
\]
Substitution into (2.9) gives
\[
 \widehat H_*(C)=
 \frac{(6+o(1))(\log C)^2}
 {12(32+o(1))(\log C)^3}
 =\frac{1+o(1)}{64\log C}.
\tag{7.5}
\]
The exact-merge predecessor is
\(H_*(C)=(1+o(1))/(96\log C)\), so the new term is eventually strictly
larger.  Because \(G_*+\widehat H_*=O(1/\log C)=o(1)\), the frozen red--blue
crossing is unchanged.  This proves \cref{thm:main-lower}.

\subsection{A narrow scalar method cap}

Consider residual arrays that satisfy all of the following: they use the same
deterministic triangular weighted-pair recursion; their increments are
nonnegative and fixed before the relevant column is sampled; they are paid by
the same scalar full-box strong-concavity inequality; and they use the same
three-factor H\"older/CGF comparison.  For such a residual, the direct leading
contribution at edge \(ij\) is
\[
 \widehat B_w(i-1)e_{ij}-\frac{e_{ij}^2}{2\mu_w}.
\tag{7.6}
\]
Pointwise optimization gives
\(e_{ij}=\mu_w\widehat B_w(i-1)=m_0^2(i-1)[1+o(1)]\).  Feedback has norm
\(\rho=o(1)\), so it changes only lower-order terms.  Thus \(1/64\) is the
leading cap for this explicitly admissible scalar class.

This is not a cap on adaptive, matrix-valued or higher-order states, on a
different source quadratic MGF, or on a different extraction invariant.
