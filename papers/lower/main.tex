\title[Controlled residuals]{Controlled Residuals in Gaussian Ramsey Constructions}
\author{Cheng Xin}
\date{Draft of 12 August 2026}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We refine the Gaussian random-graph framework for fixed-ratio Ramsey lower
bounds.  Relative to the pinned Appendix-B induction of Hunter, Milojevi\'c
and Sudakov and the exact cumulant input of Lin and Niu, we introduce a
nonexchangeable triangular weighted ledger with a deterministic boundary
residual.  Exact gradient merge uniquely fixes the triangular array and gives
only a \((96\log C)^{-1}\) leading contribution.  By retaining the full-box
strong-concavity term, we pay a controlled failure of exact merge by square
completion and obtain an additional term
\[
 \widehat H_*(C)=\frac{1+o(1)}{64\log C}>0.
\]
Under the explicit source interface (S1)--(S5), for every sufficiently large
fixed \(C\), the resulting source-relative bound is
\[
 \liminf_{\ell\to\infty}\frac1\ell
 \log R(\ell,\lfloor C\ell\rfloor)
 \ge -\frac12\log p_C+\frac{B_R(C)}2+G_*(C)+\widehat H_*(C).
\]
The threshold in \(C\) is existential and non-effective.  We also identify a
narrow scalar residual class for which the coefficient \(1/64\) is optimal;
the statement does not cover adaptive or higher-order potentials.
\end{abstract}
