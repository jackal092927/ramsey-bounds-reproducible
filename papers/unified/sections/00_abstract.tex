We present a unified reproducible account of four logically independent
results in Ramsey theory.  First, relative to version-pinned regularization,
parameterized-book, and spine-compatibility interfaces and a locally certified
six-stage off-diagonal rate theorem, an exact-diagonal correlation certificate yields
\[
 R(k,k)\le (3.780685290)^{k+o(k)},
\]
with certified unrounded base below $3.780685288379640114$.  The compact
inequalities are enclosed by 512-bit ball arithmetic and replayed by four
separately structured implementations sharing the same interval-arithmetic
trust dependency.  The positive-moment and tail transfer used there are
proved locally.  Second, after fixing the absolute source constant \(K\),
under pinned lower-interface items (S1), (S2), (S4), and (S5), with
weighted propagation (S3) proved locally, a deterministic nonexchangeable
triangular residual ledger supplies, for every sufficiently large fixed
$C$, a positive source-relative term
\[
 \widehat H_*(C)=\frac{1+o_{C\to\infty}(1)}{64\log C}
\]
in the fixed-ratio lower-bound exponent.  The threshold \(C_0(K)\) is
existential.  The coefficient \(1/64\) is a cap only within the specified
scalar residual class, and no comparison across incompatible terminal
normalizations is asserted.  Third, for a content-addressed 100-vertex
$R(3,18)$ near miss,
semantic CNF reconstruction and six checked DRAT refutations prove that its
one-sided deletion repair radius under free additions is at least seven.  The
branch-1 exact-seven refinement proves four non-vacuous singleton
consequences by four further checked DRAT refutations, excluding
$87.8832136551\ldots\%$ of raw residual supports when combined with a degree
cap.  A checked common-relaxation model contains an independent 18-set.  The
exact-seven analysis further proves a maximal-triangle-free normal form and
combines it with the complete four-bank mask union in a byte-audited CNF; its
single bounded call returned only \textsc{unknown}.  The
exact-seven layer remains \textsc{unknown}; this local theorem does not imply
$R(3,18)\ge101$.  Fourth, coherent edge-oracle access to any graph on at
least $4^{K-1}$ vertices permits a scale-aware quantum implementation of the
constructive majority recursion which returns a $K$-clique or $K$-vertex
independent set with error at most $\eta$ using
\[
 O\!\left(2^K K\log\frac K\eta\right)
\]
queries.  A random-Painter argument gives the classical randomized lower
bound $\Omega(2^{(2-\sqrt2)K})$, which does not prove a quantum--classical
separation.  An estimation-free size-biased recursion separately gives
$O(2^K K^2\log K\log(1/\eta))$ queries, admits a direct multicolour
extension, and supplies a simple survival lemma checked by exact finite
split-tree dynamic programs.  Direct parameter substitution also exposes an unresolved
conflict with a published $N^{1-o(1)}$ quantum-lower-bound remark; author
clarification remains a submission gate.  This fourth result changes the
complexity of finding a guaranteed witness, not a Ramsey-number bound.  The
common contribution is an auditable separation of
imported interfaces, analytic arguments, interval certificates,
proof-carrying finite computations, and unresolved searches, with
content-addressed artifacts and tiered reproduction entry points.
