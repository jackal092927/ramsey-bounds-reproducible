\section{Source interfaces and evidence taxonomy}\label{sec:evidence-taxonomy}

The three results have different logical dependencies.  We therefore attach
an evidence type to every principal statement instead of using
``computer-assisted'' as a single undifferentiated label.

\begin{definition}[Evidence types]
The manuscript uses the following five categories.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item A \emph{source interface} is either a version-pinned statement imported
  from an external or frozen local source or a separately identified extra
  hypothesis.  Its justification is outside the current logical kernel unless
  reproduced in an appendix.
\item An \emph{analytic theorem} follows from written mathematical arguments
  once its named source interfaces are granted.
\item An \emph{interval-certified theorem} additionally depends on
  outward-rounded enclosures covering a stated continuum.
\item A \emph{proof-carrying SAT theorem} depends on a proved encoding,
  exhaustive case coverage, and independently replayed proof traces.
\item A \emph{bounded observation} records a terminated computation without
  promoting \textsc{sat}, \textsc{unsat}, or \textsc{unknown} beyond what its
  certificate establishes.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Shared trust is not independence.}
The upper-bound replays use separately structured implementations but share
Arb or python-flint containment semantics and the same mathematical
specification.  The finite proof traces are checked independently of the SAT
solver, but their theorem still depends on the encoding and case-cover
proofs.  The lower arithmetic checker replays identities and asymptotic
margins; it does not replace the reverse-induction and extraction proof.

\paragraph{Version and hash discipline.}
All imported source snapshots, frozen parameter files, graph matrices, CNF
instances, proof traces, and checkers used as evidence are identified by
version or SHA-256 digest.  A digest authenticates bytes, not mathematics;
the relevant semantic implication is stated separately in each Part.
