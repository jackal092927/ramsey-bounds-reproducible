\section{Conclusions, non-implications, and open problems}
\label{sec:unified-conclusion}

The three Parts give complementary information about Ramsey bounds without
pretending that their techniques or normalizations coincide.  The diagonal
upper result isolates an exact-diagonal correlation gain inside a
retained-spine transfer.  The fixed-ratio lower result shows how a controlled
deterministic residual can be paid by full-box strong concavity.  The finite
result turns a near miss into a precise, proof-carrying local obstruction.

Three restrictions remain part of the conclusions.  First, the upper theorem
is source-relative and asymptotic, with no effective finite-$k$ threshold.
Second, the lower theorem is conditional on the pinned items (S1), (S2),
(S4), and (S5); weighted reverse propagation (S3) is proved locally.  After
the source constant $K$ is fixed, it fixes sufficiently large $C$ before taking
$\ell\to\infty$, then sends $w\downarrow\omega_0$ before studying
$C\to\infty$.  It has no effective $C_0(K)$ and makes no
cross-normalization priority claim.  Third, the finite theorem does not imply
$R(3,18)\ge101$.  Its four proof-checked branch-1 singleton consequences are
non-vacuous and materially strengthen the necessary support filter, but they
do not close branch 1.  A maximal-triangle-free normal form and complete
four-bank mask union give a cleaner frozen branch-1 formula, but its only
bounded endpoint is \textsc{unknown}; the exact-seven layer remains open.

The most direct next steps are correspondingly separate: strengthen the
correlation or outer partition in Part~I; seek adaptive or higher-order
residual potentials beyond the scalar class of Part~II; and either exhibit a
seven-deletion repair or produce checked exact-seven refutations in Part~III\@.
Any future improvement should retain the same separation between discovery,
human-readable implication, independently replayable certificate, and open
computational state.

Completion of the ChatGPT Pro second-opinion review does not close the
separate public-artifact gate: the final tag, immutable Release, and
credential-free final-tag replay remain pending at the date of this draft.
