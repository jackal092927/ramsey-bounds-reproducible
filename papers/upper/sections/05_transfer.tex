\section{The retained-spine certificate}\label{sec:transfer}

The exact outer tuple is
\[
 \eta=0.02868896,qquad p=0.47130887,qquad
 \delta=0.000053863,
\]
\[
 \lambda_0=13233,qquad \tau=0.00069386,qquad
 \Delta=0.0000034754.
 \tag{5.1}
\]
Every terminating decimal in (5.1) is interpreted as a rational number.

The functions \(D,B\) and \(C_*\) are those of
\cref{thm:transfer-general}.  Direct interval evaluation of the full square
would introduce unacceptable variable dependence.  The certificate instead
uses the geometry of the ordered wedge.  Concavity of \(U\) fixes the
monotonicity of each page branch; the red and blue page expressions reduce to
one-dimensional boundary intervals, and the diagonal reservoir reduces to
an endpoint inequality.  Every reduction is analytic before numerical
evaluation.

\begin{proposition}[Outer certificate]\label{prop:outer}
For the exact tuple (5.1) and the correlation input proved in
\cref{sec:exact},
\[
 C_*\le U(1)-\Delta.
 \tag{5.2}
\]
The certified lower margins are as follows.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
Inequality & lower margin\\
\midrule
degree gate & \(1.5056\cdot10^{-9}\)\\
red page & \(5.1006\cdot10^{-9}\)\\
blue page & \(3.4825\cdot10^{-6}\)\\
diagonal reservoir & \(6.0468\cdot10^{-6}\)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Partition \([0,1]^2\) into the ordered triangles
\(\sigma_R\le\sigma_B\) and \(\sigma_B\le\sigma_R\), then subdivide at
the page truncation lines.  On each analytic piece, the signs of the partial
derivatives follow from strict concavity of \(U\), reducing the maximum of
each page term to the relevant axis or diagonal boundary.  The certificate
checks both orientations and all boundary intersections with outward-rounded
balls.  It separately verifies the scalar Yang--Mao degree and reservoir
gates and the exact equality of the inner correlation constant.  The quoted
margins are the minimum lower endpoints over the complete partition.  The
machine-verifiable partition and endpoint formulas are listed in Appendix A.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{thm:main-upper}]
By \cref{thm:transfer-general,prop:outer},
\[
 R(k,k)\le \exp((U(1)-\Delta)k+o(k)).
\]
A 512-bit Arb evaluation gives
\[
 e^{U(1)-\Delta}<3.780685288380<3.780685290,
\]
with rounding margin greater than \(1.6203\cdot10^{-9}\).  This proves the
claimed safe decimal and the stronger unrounded statement.
\end{proof}
