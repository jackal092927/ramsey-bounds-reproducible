\subsection{Frozen protocol (declared before target search)}

This is a bounded attempt to add \textbf{one} descent after the frozen
fifth-stage certificate. It will not continue to a seventh stage.

The only admissible prior is the exact ten-entry target tuple in
\texttt{certificate-higher-order-decic-chain-v5.json}, whose SHA-256 is
\texttt{4ec4dbbe190a08134efe68c3a76ce4b4fb3aabdad230af703c19a28a03ecf9a9}.
No rounded, refitted, or independently retyped prior is admissible.
Before search, both existing 256-bit Arb implementations must prove this
exact prior strictly concave on all of \texttt{(0,1{]}}.

The construction search is limited to target degrees 10 through 14. A
candidate will be frozen only if all of the following predeclared gates
hold:

\begin{enumerate}

\tightlist

  base;
\item
  dense floating replay plus local scalar refinement finds unbuffered
  continuous slack at least \texttt{0.000150000000000};
\item
  the exact certificate has at most 131,072 cells and passes the

\item

\item
  exact \texttt{Decimal} comparison proves the P5 target tuple equals
  the P6 prior tuple entry by entry; and
\item
  \texttt{audit\_tests.py} passes unchanged.
\end{enumerate}

A certificate that merely has positive margins but misses one of these
gates will be reported as a frontier/FAIL, not as a new Ramsey bound.
Floating optimization and interpolation are construction aids only and
have no role in the proof.

\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}

  The sixth certificate proves one finite descent only; it supplies no
  convergence theorem for an infinite chain.
