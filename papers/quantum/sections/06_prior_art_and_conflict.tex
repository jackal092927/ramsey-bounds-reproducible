\section{Prior art and comparison with a reported lower bound}
\label{sec:prior-art}

Previous quantum papers under the phrase ``Ramsey algorithm'' solve different
problems.  Gaitan and Clark encode the existence of avoidance colourings in
an adiabatic Hamiltonian \cite{GaitanClark2012}; Wang searches the space of
$2^{\binom V2}$ labelled graphs when testing a Ramsey threshold
\cite{Wang2016}; and Pion and Mniszewski study QUBO reduction and quantum
annealing for bound discovery \cite{PionMniszewski2025}.  Their square roots
refer to the number of graph colourings, not the number of vertices of one
given graph.  D{\"o}rn's clique and independent-set query algorithms are
closer in oracle model but do not exploit the Ramsey totality promise or the
majority recursion \cite{Doern2005}.  To our knowledge, no earlier work uses
implicit-set quantum sampling to implement the constructive
Erdős--Szekeres proof; this priority statement is limited to the exact oracle
model and sources compared here.

The closest exact-model source is Jain--Li--Robere--Xun
\cite{JainLiRobereXun2024}.  Their formal definitions use $N=2^n$ vertices
and default target $K=n/2$, and their query-complexity paragraph states an
$N^{1-o(1)}$ quantum lower bound via a graph-hash reduction and the
Liu--Zhandry multicollision theorem \cite{LiuZhandry2019}.  The hard instances
are symmetric valid graphs, while \cref{cor:unpromised} also aligns our upper
bound with the locally verifiable arbitrary-circuit relation.

For collision range size $B$ and fixed multiplicity $t$, the reduction has
$M\asymp B^{4t}$, whereas Liu--Zhandry's exponent in $B$ is
$\alpha_t=(2^{t-1}-1)/(2^t-1)$.  The inherited exponent is therefore
\[
 \frac{\alpha_t}{4t}
 =\frac{2^{t-1}-1}{4t(2^t-1)}\le\frac1{24},
 \qquad t\ge2,
\]
with equality at $t=2$.  Thus these two ingredients do not yield
$M^{1-o(1)}$.

The upper theorem and the printed near-linear lower cannot both hold in the
same model.  We have found no public correction.  We leave clarification of
the discrepancy as an open issue and make no claim about the intended
parameterization.
