\section{Derivation of the randomized classical lower bound}
\label{app:classical-lower}

We derive \cref{prop:classical-lower} from the random-Painter weight bound of
Conlon--Fox--Grinshpun--He \cite{ConlonFoxGrinshpunHe2019}.  Let
\[
 \beta=2-\sqrt2,
 \qquad
 \alpha=1-\frac1{\sqrt2},
\]
so that $\beta=2\alpha$.  Set
\[
 T=\left\lfloor2^{\beta K-2}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad
 c=\lfloor\alpha K\rfloor=\alpha K+\epsilon,
 \qquad -1<\epsilon\le0.
\]

Expose $G(M,1/2)$ adaptively to a deterministic $T$-query algorithm.  The
cited random-Painter estimate bounds the probability that the queried edges
already contain a fully exposed monochromatic $K$-set by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:random-painter-bound}
 2\cdot
 2^{-\binom K2+c(c-1)}(2T)^{K-c}.
\end{equation}
Because $2T\le2^{\beta K-1}$, the base-$2$ logarithm of
\cref{eq:random-painter-bound} is at most
\[
 1-\binom K2+c(c-1)+(K-c)(\beta K-1)
 =1-\frac K2+\epsilon^2.
\]
For $K\ge8$ this is at most $-2$, so the event has probability at most
$1/4$.

If the algorithm outputs a $K$-set that is not already a fully exposed
monochromatic set, then, conditional on the transcript, either its known
edges already conflict or at least one independent fair edge remains to be
guessed.  Its conditional probability of being homogeneous is therefore at
most $1/2$.  The total success probability under $G(M,1/2)$ is at most
\[
 \frac14+\frac34\cdot\frac12=\frac58<\frac23.
\]
Yao's minimax principle now implies that any randomized algorithm with
worst-case success probability at least $2/3$ needs more than $T$ queries.
Since $M=4^{K-1}$,
\[
 2^{\beta K}
 =\Theta\!\left(M^{\beta/2}\right)
 =\Theta\!\left(M^{1-1/\sqrt2}\right),
\]
which proves \cref{prop:classical-lower}.
